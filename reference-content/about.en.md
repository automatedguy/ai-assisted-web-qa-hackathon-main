# Nimbus — About (source, en-US)

## Our story
Nimbus started with a simple idea: your files should be safe, synced, and easy to find, no matter where you are. Today, thousands of teams trust Nimbus to keep their work backed up and accessible.

## Our products
Our products are fast and reliable. NimbusSync keeps every device in step. NimbusBox, our home backup appliance, weighs just 1 kilogram, so it fits neatly on any desk.

## Our principles
What guides our work.

- **Clarity from the start.** We want every decision to make work simpler for the people who use Nimbus.
- **Care in the details.** We pay attention to everyday tasks: saving an idea, finding a document, and continuing a project.
- **Listening to improve.** We believe understanding different ways of working is the first step toward building something useful.
